Telegram answers with the channel's total message count. We fetch the new messages first, then compare what we hold (including messages from previous visits) against what Telegram reports. If the two counts match, nothing was deleted anywhere in that channel. If the count is short, the gap says exactly how many vanished, and only then do we pay for a wide scan, walking backwards until each one is found. To catch edits, in the case of no deletions we re-fetch the last **N** messages, while in the other case we piggyback off the re-fetch needed to find the deleted messages. There is a trade-off: we lose exact timestamps and get detection time instead. In exchange, twelve thousand channels, not thirteen.
