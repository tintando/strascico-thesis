Prior corpora are snapshots: a scraper walks each channel once, prints done, and stops. Whatever changes afterwards is invisible. One way to capture these changes is Telegram's live update stream, which requires an account that joins every channel it watches and stays connected. One predecessor used this approach, Kireev and colleagues, they received new messages live from thirteen channels, albeit ignoring the edit and deletion notifications. To watch thousands of channels, you cannot join them all.
