Everything so far was the machine. This is its output: a corpus of public channels, and on top of it the change layers no snapshot has: detected deletions, edits with the text before and after, changing restriction flags and a channel metadata snapshot at every visit. And note the poll: most of the corpus is not channel posts but comments. Telegram builds comments sideways: behind the channel sits a hidden discussion group, a separate chat altogether; every post is forwarded there automatically, and its replies are the comments. The scraper recurses into that group, so the comment side gets exactly the same treatment.
