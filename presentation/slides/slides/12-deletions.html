Deletions are a different story. Two point four million messages vanished under our watch. Comments die four times as often as posts, and most deleted comments go in bulk, when an entire thread is purged, for example as a result of a broadcast post being deleted. Deleted content is about one point six times more toxic than what survives. Individually deleted messages run far more toxic than those removed in thread purges.
