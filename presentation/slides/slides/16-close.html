So, *three contributions*. A system: a scraper that recovers edits and deletions without staying connected, by revisiting and diffing. A corpus: nearly half a billion messages with three change layers, including a channel metadata snapshot at every visit. And a showcase: what we just saw. The system inherently compromises: detection intervals are more or less tight, snowball bias is difficult to correct, and corpus rates are not platform prevalence. And the future work writes itself, because the scraper is still running: sustained running turns samples into dynamics. The deletion sample becomes moderation dynamics, the edit archive becomes a history of corrections, and the comment side becomes audience dynamics: who comments, who returns, and how reactions shift over time. And that's it, Thank you.
