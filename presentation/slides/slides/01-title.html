I'm Gabriel Bustamante, and with Professor Alessandro Mei and co-Advisor Alberto Mongardini, we worked on Making Telegram's content dynamics observable, through edits, deletions, and restrictions. This is **Telegram**: it's half messenger, half broadcast medium, where much of the world follows news, wars, and protests live in public channels. It's also found infamy, both in literature and media, as a host of conspiracy, scams, and extremism.
