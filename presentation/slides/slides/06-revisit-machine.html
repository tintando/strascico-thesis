Our answer is the revisit machine. It visits public channels over and over, forever. Every visit fetches new messages, snapshots the channel's metadata, and walks into the linked discussion group where the comments live. What changed since the last visit is recovered by diffing, and how that stays affordable, I will show in a moment. The corpus grows on its own: when a post is forwarded from a channel we have never seen, the scraper follows the forward. Discovery snowballs, one forward at a time.
